#Oggy and the Cockroaches is a French animated series about a blue cat, Oggy, whose life is constantly disrupted by three mischievous cockroaches Oggy and the Cockroaches (French: Oggy et les Cafards) is an animated television series created by Jean-Yves Raimbaud and produced by Gaumont Multimedia for season 1 and Xilam Animation for seasons 2–8 (in co-production with Les Productions Coquerelles for season 2). It chronicles the comedic exploits and burlesque adventures of a rotund, blue cat named Oggy, whose days are always ruined by three cockroaches – Joey, Marky, and Dee Dee respectively – who regularly cause mischief at his home.
